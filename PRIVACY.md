# Selenite Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Selenite is a client for Sunshine game streaming hosts on your own network. It does not collect, share or sell any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your Apple TV

- **Your hosts:** the name, network address and MAC address of each Sunshine host you add, so Selenite can find it again and wake it from sleep.
- **Pairing:** a client certificate and key that Selenite creates when you pair with a host, kept in the Apple TV keychain, together with the certificate of each paired host.
- **Settings:** your streaming settings, per-host overrides, your last split-screen setup and the list of recently played games.

This data is stored only on your Apple TV. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party, and it is removed when you delete the app.

## Network connections

Selenite connects only to the Sunshine hosts on your network that you choose: to discover them, pair with them, list their games and stream from them. The stream runs directly between your Apple TV and your host. Selenite does not connect to any server run by the developer.

## Children

Selenite does not collect data from anyone, including children.
